About the App

Our application leverages the power of AI to analyze resumes, match candidates with suitable job openings, and facilitate the interview process. It provides a comprehensive platform for job seekers and recruiters alike.

Key features include:

  • Resume analysis using AI.
  • Job recommendations based on CV.
  • Admin tools for managing candidates and jobs.
  • Interview scheduling and AI/human interviews.
